The story of how our
company
was founded

Founded by a passionate group of visionary engineers in a modest workshop, we began with a single blast furnace and a bold mission to redefine structural integrity. Through decades of relentless dedication, innovation, and strategic partnerships, we have successfully evolved into a globally recognized leader in heavy industrial fabrication.

02

Brands & companies
we worked with

Construct X

Providing high-tensile steel girders and custom load-bearing support structures for state-of-the-art commercial skyscrapers and residential high-rises across North America.

Cargo X

Engineering lightweight, exceptionally corrosion-resistant aluminum alloy components for modern maritime logistics, massive cargo vessels, and deep-sea freight containers.

Carpenters X

Supplying specialized CNC machined mechanical parts and advanced thermal treatment services to empower independent manufacturing facilities globally.

everything we do

Our core principles heavily shape our workplace culture, guide our critical engineering decisions, and ensure we consistently deliver unparalleled excellence.

Quality

We maintain rigorous QA protocols, ensuring every manufactured component surpasses international industry standards.

Innovation
